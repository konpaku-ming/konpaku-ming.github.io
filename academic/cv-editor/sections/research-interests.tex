\VectraSection{RESEARCH INTERESTS}

\VectraResearchInterests{
My research interests lie in machine learning for audio. I currently focus on automatic speech recognition in multi-speaker settings, with a broader interest in developing general-purpose audio models for understanding and generation. My long-term goal is to develop such models based on unified and structured audio representations that jointly capture high-level semantic content and fine-grained acoustic information, providing a shared foundation for both audio understanding and high-fidelity generation.
}
