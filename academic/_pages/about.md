I am **Xuanming Ding (丁宣铭)**, a third-year undergraduate student in the [ACM Honors Class](https://acm.sjtu.edu.cn/home) at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/), majoring in Computer Science and Technology.

My research interests lie in speech and audio processing, with a current focus on multi-speaker speech recognition. In the long term, I aim to develop general-purpose audio models built on unified, structured representations that capture both high-level semantics and fine-grained acoustic detail, supporting audio understanding and high-fidelity generation.

Feel free to check out my [CV](/home/files/CV.pdf) or [send me an email](mailto:dxuanming@sjtu.edu.cn) to chat about research or potential collaborations.
